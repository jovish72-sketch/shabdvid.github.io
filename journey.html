THE JOURNEY

Every Chapter Shapes The Story.

Every milestone in life became a stepping stone toward creativity, storytelling and meaningful communication. Here's the journey that shaped the vision behind Shabdvid.

1992

Born In Jamnagar, Gujarat

A journey began in the culturally rich city of Jamnagar, where curiosity, creativity and a deep interest in learning started taking shape.

School

Primary Education

Brilliant School, Jamnagar, Gujarat.

SGVP

Traditional Sanskrit Education

Darshanam Sanskrit Mahavidyalaya, SGVP, Charodi, Ahmedabad, Gujarat.

B.A. • M.A.

Nyaya Darshan

Bachelor's (Shastri) and Master's (Acharya) in Indian Philosophy & Logic from Shree Somnath Sanskrit University.

First Career

Librarian

Began the professional journey as a Librarian at Shree Jain Mahavir Aradhana Kendra, Koba, Gandhinagar, where research, reading and knowledge became an integral part of everyday life.

Media

Garv Shree Swaminarayan TV

Entered the media industry as Writer, Anchor, Voice Artist, Reporter and Project Head, laying the foundation for a creative career.

Television

Sadvidya TV

Served as Program Head, Writer, Anchor and Voice Artist while creating meaningful television content.

Production

Shriji Films (Gujju Production)

Worked as Writer, Executive Producer, Program Head and Anchor, contributing to creative productions and entertainment projects.

National TV

Doordarshan Kendra Ahmedabad

Served DD News Gujarati as Writer, Copywriter and Anchor, contributing to public broadcasting and news communication.

Recognition

Jury Member

Invited to serve as Jury Member at the Ahmedabad International Film Festival, recognizing creative excellence in cinema.

Leadership

Dhudiya Entertainment

Serving as Head of Distribution, Sales & Licensing, managing strategic partnerships and content distribution.

Present

Founder — Shabdvid

Leading Shabdvid as an independent Creative Writing Studio, offering premium writing services, storytelling and creative consultancy for brands, governments and creators.
