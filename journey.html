THE JOURNEY

Every Chapter Shapes The Story.

Every milestone in life became a stepping stone toward creativity, storytelling and meaningful communication. Here's the journey that shaped the vision behind Shabdvid.

CHAPTER 01

The Researcher

Shree Jain Mahavir Aradhana Kendra

Before stepping into media, I spent my early professional years surrounded by books, knowledge and research. Working as a Librarian strengthened my habit of observation, discipline and lifelong learning— qualities that later became the foundation of my writing.

CHAPTER 02

The Broadcaster

Garv Shree Swaminarayan TV

My media journey truly began here. As a Writer, Anchor, Voice Artist, Reporter and Project Head, I discovered the power of storytelling beyond the written word.

CHAPTER 03

Finding My Voice

Sadvidya Television

This chapter refined my creative vision. Leading programs while writing and presenting content taught me how stories inspire, educate and connect with audiences.

CHAPTER 04

Lights. Camera. Stories.

Shriji Films (Gujju Production)

My journey expanded into the world of production, where I contributed as a Writer, Executive Producer, Program Head and Anchor. This chapter deepened my understanding of filmmaking, visual storytelling and creative leadership.

CHAPTER 05

National Television

Doordarshan Kendra Ahmedabad

Joining DD News Gujarati marked an important milestone in my professional journey. As a Writer, Copywriter and Anchor, I had the opportunity to create content for India's national public broadcaster while reaching a diverse audience across Gujarat.

CHAPTER 06

Recognition Beyond Writing

Ahmedabad International Film Festival

Serving as a Jury Member at the Ahmedabad International Film Festival was a proud recognition of my creative journey. It offered the opportunity to evaluate cinematic excellence while engaging with filmmakers from around the world.

CHAPTER 07

Building Creative Partnerships

Dhudiya Entertainment

As Head of Distribution, Sales & Licensing, I continue to collaborate with creators, producers and businesses, building meaningful partnerships and expanding the reach of quality content across platforms.

PRESENT

The Story Continues...

Founder – Shabdvid

Today, I lead Shabdvid as an independent creative writing studio dedicated to scripts, documentaries, films, books, speeches, lyrics and strategic storytelling. Every project is an opportunity to transform ideas into meaningful narratives that inspire, preserve legacies and create lasting impact. The journey continues—one story at a time.
