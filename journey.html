PROFESSIONAL JOURNEY

Where Knowledge Became Storytelling.

Education laid the foundation, but the real transformation began when knowledge met creativity. Every workplace, every responsibility and every opportunity added a new chapter to a journey dedicated to meaningful storytelling.

01
CHAPTER 01

The Researcher

Shree Jain Mahavir Aradhana Kendra

Before stepping into media, I spent my early professional years surrounded by books, knowledge and research. Working as a Librarian strengthened my habit of observation, discipline and lifelong learning— qualities that later became the foundation of my writing.

"Every writer is first a reader."
02
CHAPTER 02

The Broadcaster

Garv Shree Swaminarayan TV

This chapter marked the beginning of my media journey. As a Writer, Anchor, Voice Artist, Reporter and Project Head, I discovered how ideas evolve into stories and stories evolve into meaningful experiences. It was here that my passion for visual storytelling truly found its direction.

“The camera captures moments, but stories make them eternal.”
03
CHAPTER 03

Finding My Voice

Sadvidya Television

Every project became an opportunity to learn, lead and create. Working as Program Head, Writer, Anchor and Voice Artist strengthened my belief that authentic storytelling can educate, inspire and create lasting emotional connections.

“Leadership begins by giving every story a meaningful voice.”
04
CHAPTER 04

Lights. Camera. Stories.

Shriji Films (Gujju Production)

Stepping into production expanded my perspective beyond writing. As Writer, Executive Producer, Program Head and Anchor, I learned how every department contributes to a powerful narrative and how collaboration transforms ideas into memorable visual experiences.

“Every frame becomes timeless when it carries a meaningful story.”
05
CHAPTER 05

The National Stage

Doordarshan Kendra Ahmedabad

Joining DD News Gujarati marked a defining chapter in my journey. Serving as a Writer, Copywriter and Anchor for India's national public broadcaster refined my ability to communicate with clarity, responsibility and purpose while reaching audiences across Gujarat.

“Words carry greater power when they serve the public with truth.”
06
CHAPTER 06

Recognition Beyond Writing

Ahmedabad International Film Festival

Being invited as a Jury Member at the Ahmedabad International Film Festival was a meaningful recognition of my creative journey. It provided the privilege of evaluating cinematic excellence while engaging with filmmakers and storytellers from across the world.

“Recognition is not the destination; it is the encouragement to create more.”
07
CHAPTER 07

Building Creative Partnerships

Dhudiya Entertainment

As Head of Distribution, Sales & Licensing, I continue to collaborate with creators, producers and businesses to expand the reach of meaningful content. This chapter is about connecting ideas, building partnerships and creating opportunities for stories to travel farther.

“Every meaningful partnership begins with a shared vision.”
08
PRESENT

The Story Continues...

Founder • Shabdvid

Today, I lead Shabdvid as an independent creative writing studio dedicated to scripts, documentaries, feature films, books, lyrics, speeches and strategic storytelling. Every project is an opportunity to transform ideas into narratives that inspire, preserve legacies and create meaningful impact. The next chapter is already being written—one story at a time.

“Every ending is simply the beginning of another story.”
